Cyber incidents in Japan

Publicly reported cybersecurity incidents affecting Japanese organizations, collected from news, security publications, and official notices.

This index is collected automatically and analyzed with AI. It may contain omissions or errors and should not be regarded as a complete or authoritative record. Verify details with the linked sources.

Confidence is a source-based score, not incident severity, an AI certainty rating, or a probability of accuracy. The highest source tier sets the base: official/government 60, news/security press 50, aggregator 35, other 30, and social media 10 (no sources: 0). Each additional distinct non-social domain adds 15 points, capped at 100; search/news wrapper URLs use the recorded outlet name instead. High: 60–100; Medium: 30–59; Low: 0–29. Tiers are assigned automatically from domains, outlet names and notice-style titles/URLs and may be misclassified. Different domains can repeat the same report, so higher scores do not guarantee independent confirmation. Even 100 does not mean 100% accuracy. Verify the linked sources.

Date shows the incident date, or the reported date when the incident date is unavailable.
