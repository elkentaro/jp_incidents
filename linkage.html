Reading the map

Orange marks upstream incidents, blue downstream incidents, and purple unlinked incidents with no recorded connection. Teal marks incidents with both upstream and downstream roles; charcoal marks other related incidents. A missing connection does not establish that two incidents are unrelated.

About this snapshot

The static image is refreshed when published incidents or relationships change, and at least every 24 hours during normal publication. Larger upstream clusters have extra spacing to keep their victims apart. Names, incident IDs, source links and incident dates are omitted; the connection pattern is retained. It is an overview of recorded relationships, not a complete map of cyber incidents in Japan.
